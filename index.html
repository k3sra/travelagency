Why FABLE

Four things we will not compromise on.Four things we will not compromise on.

We built the trip we could not find: small, hosted, honest about the price, and easy to walk away from if it is not for you.

  1. 01

    Twelve people. Never more.

    Big enough for a boat day, small enough to sit at one table. Most weeks are half solo travellers, half pairs. Nobody arrives knowing everyone; everybody leaves knowing everyone.

  2. 02

    A host who actually lives there.

    Every week is run by one host who has lived in that place for years. They book the tables, know the boat captains, and know when to leave you alone.

  3. 03

    The price is the price.

    Every night, every meal at the table, every drink at the table, every transfer, every activity on the itinerary. You pay for souvenirs and whatever you order after we have gone to bed.

  4. 04

    Change your mind for free.

    Your deposit is refundable in full for fourteen days, no reason required. After that it moves to any other week within a year.
